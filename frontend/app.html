Standard LLMs suffer from complete session amnesia—they forget past pricing caps, previous objections, and compliance redlines. DealMind provides an autonomous intelligence layer backed by Vectorize Hindsight persistent episodic memory, eliminating fatal pricing mistakes and accelerating deal velocity by 45%.
